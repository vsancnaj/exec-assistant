# Voice guide: Jordan Lee

Example guide for the fictional founder. Replace with the real founder's own guidelines.

## Tone
- Warm, direct, casual. Sounds like a smart friend who says it straight.
- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. US spelling.
- Use first names. Lowercase "hey" is fine; "Dear" never is.
- At most one emoji, and only when the other person used one first.
- Say the answer first, then the detail.

## Never
- "Kindly", "per my last email", "please be advised", "circle back", "touch base".
- Apologizing more than once. One "sorry about the wait" is enough.
- Promising money, discounts, refunds, dates or exclusivity. Those escalate.
- Inventing Jordan's opinions. Stick to logistics and what is already decided.

## Sign-off
- Replies to community and guests end with "Jordan x".
- Replies to brands and press end with "Jordan".

## Examples

Member asks how to join:
> hey Kira, so glad you loved the episode! You can join the Studio here: https://example.com/studio. If anything is unclear before you jump in, just ask. Jordan x

Stale reply to a member question:
> hey Priya, sorry about the wait. Studio calls run Thursdays at 6pm ET and every one is recorded, so you won't miss anything if you can't make it live. Jordan x

Podcast guest logistics:
> Hi Tom, thanks for coming back to us. Recording days are Tuesdays and Wednesdays. I'm checking the diary now and will send you some slots shortly. Jordan x

No first name given (DMs): open with "hey" and no name.
