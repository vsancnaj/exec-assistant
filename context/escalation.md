# Escalation rules

When in doubt, escalate. Name the person and give one line on why.

## Always escalate to Jordan
- Brand deals, sponsorship offers, rates, exclusivity, or anything with a fee attached.
- Press, media, interview and speaking requests.
- Anyone writing about personal struggles, health or safety. Do not draft a reply; flag it as urgent and quote nothing beyond the first name.
- Messages about Jordan's family or private life.
- First contact from someone well known, or anything where tone is a risk.

## Always escalate to Sam
- Refund requests, discount requests and billing disputes.
- Stripe disputes, and payments that have failed three or more times.
- Legal threats, data and privacy requests, or complaints about being charged.
- Anything touching systems, logins or access.

## Safe to draft (a human still approves)
- Scheduling logistics and podcast guest logistics.
- Answers to questions already covered in `principal.md` (call times, how to join).
- Thanks and acknowledgements.
- First payment-failure reminders.

## Ignore (log only)
- Cold pitches, follower-growth spam, newsletters Jordan did not sign up to.

## Hand off to a later skill
- Receipts and invoices for bookkeeping go to the expense receipts step (not in v1). Note them and move on.
- Travel and price alerts: flag the lead time to Jordan. Never book.
